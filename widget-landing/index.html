Move money in one sentence.

Swap across chains, cash out to your bank, or buy crypto with a bank transfer. Type what you want; RecoilPay finds a solver and settles it.

Swap

Any supported chain to any other. A solver delivers on the destination chain from its own funds.

Cash out

USDC to a Nigerian bank account. Your USDC waits in escrow until you confirm the naira arrived.

Buy

Pay naira by bank transfer, receive USDC. The solver's USDC is locked for you before you're shown where to pay.
